# Brand Guidelines

## Brand promise
Talent Ladder helps workers reach high-value technical careers with a clear path, not guesswork.

## Voice and tone
- Direct, practical, hopeful
- Avoid jargon-heavy copy
- Emphasize momentum, credibility, and outcomes

## Messaging pillars
1. **Clarity:** clear steps from current role to target role
2. **Access:** grants and funding reduce financial barriers
3. **Opportunity:** real jobs and wage progression visibility
4. **Trust:** transparent data handling and responsible onboarding

## UX content rules
- Use plain-language CTAs (e.g., “Find My Career Path”).
- Include concrete outcomes (salary ranges, timing, milestones).
- Keep forms concise; progressively disclose advanced fields.
